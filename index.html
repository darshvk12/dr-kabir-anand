(About) 001

Your heart beats about a hundred thousand times a day. Most of them you never notice. My work is to keep it that way: find what is wrong early, fix only what needs fixing, and tell you the truth at every step.

The short version

I trained in Mumbai and London, and have spent two decades in catheterisation labs treating blocked arteries and failing valves through an incision the width of a pencil tip. I now lead the structural heart programme at Meridian Heart Institute.

How I practise

Not every blockage needs a stent, and not every chest pain is the heart. You will leave each appointment knowing what we found, what the options are, what I would choose for my own family, and why.

    For
    Angina, heart attack, failed bypass grafts
    Procedure
    45 to 120 minutes, local anaesthetic
    Recovery
    Home in 6 to 24 hours

  • Replacing a narrowed aortic valve and closing holes in the heart through a vessel in the leg, without opening the chest or stopping the heart.

    For
    Aortic stenosis, ASD, PFO, mitral leaks
    Procedure
    60 to 90 minutes, conscious sedation
    Recovery
    Home in 2 to 3 days
